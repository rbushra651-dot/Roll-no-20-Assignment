Dish Descriptions

Siri Paye
A traditional slow-cooked dish made with beef or goat trotters and head, served with fresh naan.
Chicken Karahi
A spicy curry cooked in a wok-like pan with fresh tomatoes, ginger, and green chilies.
Halwa Puri
A popular breakfast platter containing crispy deep-fried bread, sweet halwa, and spicy chickpea curry.
