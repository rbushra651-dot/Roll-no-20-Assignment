What are these Dishes?

Nihari

A rich, slow-cooked stew made with meat and a blend of spices, typically served with naan or rice.

Lahori Chargha

A whole chicken marinated in a mixture of spices, deep-fried to perfection, and served with chutney and naan.

Halwa Puri

A popular breakfast platter containing crispy deep-fried bread, sweet halwa, and spicy chickpea curry.
